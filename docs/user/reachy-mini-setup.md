# Set up a Reachy Mini with MaiPai

You need a Reachy Mini, its power supply, your home Wi-Fi name and password, and a MaiPai Home that is already running.

This page takes you from the box to your first conversation. It also tells you plainly what this robot cannot do.

## Steps

1. Take the robot out of the box and plug it in.

   You will see the robot wake up and move its head.

2. Connect the robot to your home Wi-Fi.

   Use the Wi-Fi setup that Pollen Robotics put in the box with the robot. MaiPai does not change this step. When it finishes, the robot is on the same network as your MaiPai Home.

3. Open MaiPai Home on your computer and choose **Devices**.

   You will see the list of devices your home already uses.

4. Select **Add a robot**.

   You will see the robots Home found on your network. This can take up to a minute.

5. Pick your Reachy Mini from the list.

   Home asks you to confirm that the charger is plugged in. The robot cannot tell Home this itself, so Home asks you. Plug it in, then confirm.

6. Listen for the robot to say a short code.

   The robot speaks the code out loud, one character at a time. It has no screen, so it cannot show it.

7. Type the code into Home.

   If you missed it, ask Home to start again and the robot will say it again.

8. Wait while Home sets up the robot.

   Home installs the MaiPai software on the robot over your home network. It also changes the robot's starting password to a random one and keeps that password safe for you. You will see each step tick off, and then the robot appears in your Devices list.

9. Say "Hey MaiPai" and ask something.

   You will see the robot turn toward you and the antennas lift. After a moment it answers out loud.

## What the antennas mean

The robot has no lights and no screen, so its antennas and its head show what it is doing.

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| Both antennas forward and a little up, head turned toward you | It is listening |
| Antennas soft and low, head holding still | It is thinking |
| Antennas moving with its voice | It is speaking |
| Both antennas straight down and not moving | It is muted |
| Antennas soft and low once, then still, and the head stays where it is | It has been lifted or carried |

Your robot's card in Home shows the same states in words: listening, thinking, speaking and muted. If you cannot see the robot, look at the card.

## What this robot cannot do

These limits are real. They come from the robot, not from a setting you missed.

- **Mute is a software switch.** When you mute the robot, MaiPai stops using the microphone and the antennas drop. The microphone is still powered. There is no button that cuts it off.
- **Camera off is a watch level, not a shutter.** You can tell the robot not to watch. The lens stays uncovered, so if you want it blind, turn the robot to face a wall or unplug it.
- **No battery reading yet.** Home cannot read how full the battery is. It shows "level unknown", or one of three rough levels once those have been measured.
- **No screen.** The robot cannot show a code, a picture or a message. It speaks, moves its head and antennas, and uses its card in Home.
- **It needs your Home to think.** The robot answers by asking your Home. If Home is off or out of reach, the robot says so once and waits.

## Fix a problem

- **Home does not find the robot:** Check that the robot and your computer are on the same Wi-Fi. Unplug the robot, plug it in, wait a minute and choose **Add a robot** again.
- **You did not hear the code:** Ask Home to start the code again. The robot will say it again.
- **The code is rejected:** Ask Home for a new code and type the new one.
- **Setup stops and says to plug in the charger:** Plug the robot in and confirm again.
- **The robot does not move when you speak:** Look at the antennas. If they hang straight down, the robot is muted. Unmute it from its card in Home.

## What leaves the robot

Only conversations with your own Home. The full list of what the robot connects to, and how to check it, is on the [privacy page](reachy-mini-privacy.md).
